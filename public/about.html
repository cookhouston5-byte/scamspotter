About

A scam checker built in Alaska, for the people who pay the bills.

ScamSpotter is a small, independent business run by Houston Cook in Anchorage.

Why ScamSpotter exists

At most small businesses, there’s one person who pays the invoices, runs payroll, and answers the inbox, usually while doing three other jobs. Scammers know that. They send messages that look like a real vendor, a real boss, or a real bank, and they push for money or a password before anyone has time to think.

I built ScamSpotter to give that person a second pair of eyes. Paste the message, and within seconds you see whether it looks safe, suspicious, or like a scam, with the reasons spelled out in plain English and a clear next step.
