Privacy Policy

Last updated November 3, 2026

ScamSpotter is a small business based in Anchorage, Alaska. This page explains what we collect when you use scamspotter.app and what we do with it. The short version: we don’t save the messages you check, and we don’t sell anything about you.

Messages you check

When you paste a message into the checker, it is sent to our server, analyzed, and the result is sent back to you. We do not save the message text, sender, or subject. If AI analysis is turned on, the text is also sent to our AI provider (OpenAI) to produce a second opinion. OpenAI processes it under its API data policy, which does not use API data to train its models by default.

Please don’t paste passwords, full card numbers, or other secrets into the checker.

Free check limit

Free use is limited to a few checks per day. To enforce this, our server keeps a temporary count of checks per IP address for the current day, in memory only. It is cleared daily and whenever the server restarts.

Feedback and reviews you send

If you use the feedback form or leave a review, we store what you write, the time, and the name, business, and email you choose to give us. We also store a short one-way hash of your IP address to block spam, and we send a copy to our own inbox so we can reply. Reviews are only published on the site after we approve them, showing your name, business (if given), rating, and comment.

Payments and your team key

Subscriptions are handled by Stripe. Your card details go directly to Stripe and never touch our servers. Stripe shares your name, email, and subscription status with us so we can run your plan. Your team access key is linked to your Stripe customer record; it is saved in your browser when you enter it, and sent with each check so we can confirm your plan. See Stripe’s privacy policy.

Email

We send account emails (like your welcome email and team key) through our email provider, Resend. We don’t send marketing email without your permission.

Technical data

Like most websites, our hosting providers (Render and Cloudflare) process IP addresses and basic request logs to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when it delivers them. We don’t use advertising trackers or sell personal information. Your theme choice and team key are saved in your own browser’s storage, not on our servers.

How long we keep things

Checked messages: not kept. Daily check counts: until the end of the day. Feedback and reviews: until we no longer need them. Billing records: as long as required for tax and accounting.

Your choices

You can ask us to see or delete the information we hold about you. Email hello@scamspotter.app.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top of this page.
