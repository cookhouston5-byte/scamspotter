Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2, 2026

ScamSpotter is a small business based in Anchorage, Alaska. This page explains what we collect when you use scamspotter.app and what we do with it. The short version: we don't save the messages you check, and we don't sell anything about you.

Messages you check

When you paste a message into the checker, it is sent to our server, analyzed, and the result is sent back to you. We do not save the message text, sender, or subject. If AI analysis is turned on, the text is also sent to our AI provider (OpenAI) to produce a second opinion. OpenAI processes it under its API data policy, which does not use API data to train its models by default.

Please don't paste passwords, full card numbers, or other secrets into the checker.

Feedback you send

If you use the feedback form, we store your message, the page you sent it from, the time, and the name and email you choose to give us (both optional). We also store a short one-way hash of your IP address to block spam. We use this only to read and reply to your feedback.

Payments

Subscriptions are handled by Stripe. Your card details go directly to Stripe and never touch our servers. Stripe shares your name, email, and subscription status with us so we can set up your account. See Stripe's privacy policy.

Technical data

Like most websites, our hosting providers (Render and Cloudflare) process IP addresses and basic request logs to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. We use IP addresses briefly, in memory, to limit how many checks one visitor can run. We don't use advertising trackers or sell personal information.

How long we keep things

Checked messages: not kept. Feedback: until we no longer need it to respond or improve the product. Billing records: as long as required for tax and accounting.

Your choices

You can ask us to see or delete the feedback or account information we hold about you. Send a request through the feedback form and include an email we can reply to.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
