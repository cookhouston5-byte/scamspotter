What it targets

The scams aimed at small businesses

Tuned for the messages that reach the people who pay bills, run payroll, and answer the front desk.

Phishing & fake logins

“Your Microsoft 365 password expires today.” A link to a fake sign-in page.

Lookalike links · spoofed brands · “verify your account”

Fake invoices

Bills for work you never ordered, or a real vendor’s invoice sent by someone else.

Overdue pressure · risky attachments · mismatched senders

Payment-change fraud

“We’ve changed banks.” One reply can redirect a large payment for good.

New bank details · wires · crypto · deposit changes

Gift card scams

A text “from the owner” asking staff to buy gift cards quickly and quietly.

Gift card asks · secrecy · free email accounts

Fake support & renewals

“Your subscription renewed for $499. Call to cancel.” The number reaches a scammer.

Callback numbers · remote-access tools · surprise charges

Delivery & account texts

“Your package is on hold. Update your address here.”

Risky domains · short links · urgent deadlines

FAQ

Questions

How accurate is it?

ScamSpotter looks for the warning signs that show up again and again in real business scams. It catches a lot, but no tool is perfect. Treat “Safe” as one input, not a guarantee. For anything involving money or passwords, verify through a channel you trust.

Do you store the messages we check?

No. The checker analyzes the message to produce a result and doesn’t save the message content. If optional AI analysis is turned on, the text is sent to that AI provider for the check. Don’t paste passwords or full card numbers.

How does my team get unlimited checks?

When you start the trial, you get a team access key on screen and by email. Share it with your staff. Each person pastes it once under Menu → Your plan, and that browser gets unlimited checks. No individual accounts needed.

How do I check on or cancel my subscription?

Open the Menu (top right) and go to Your plan. It shows whether you’re on the trial, when it ends, and your next renewal date. Tap “Manage subscription” to update your card, download invoices, or cancel.

Can staff forward emails instead of pasting them?

A forwarding inbox (forward a message, get the verdict by reply) is in the works and will be included in the Team plan. Today, staff paste the message into the checker.
